## SUMMARY

**NVIDIA** ([NVDA](https://www.fool.com/quote/nasdaq/nvda/) -14.01%) reported total revenue of $115.8 billion for the third quarter and announced a leadership transition following the resignation of its long-term chief executive. Management raised the fiscal year 2028 revenue growth guidance to approximately 75% and reported that the Vera Rubin platform generated nearly $20 billion in its first quarter of production shipments. The company is expanding its global supply chain through new advanced packaging and substrate agreements with Kinsus, Samsung Electro-Mechanics, and SPIL to address sustained demand for AI infrastructure. Additionally, NVIDIA announced a definitive agreement to acquire Hugging Face and established a National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee to manage engagement with the U.S. government.

- Chairman Jones noted that the Board unanimously accepted the resignation of Jensen Huang and "does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO" while starting a search with Heidrick & Struggles for a permanent successor.
- Colette Kress stated, "Hopper GPUs have recontracted at up to twice their original price under the multiyear agreements, while the B300 rates have risen more than 60% since their introduction."
- The company launched the Open Agent Safety Platform to provide silicon-level security controls for agentic AI workloads, which now account for 80% of tokens on OpenRouter.
- Management introduced the DSX POD reference architecture, a modular design that cuts data center deployment times from months to weeks and can generate $50 billion in annual revenue per gigawatt factory.
- The company reported that hyperscale cloud industry backlog reached $2.5 trillion, as hyperscaler cloud revenue growth accelerated for the sixth consecutive quarter.
- NVIDIA confirmed that it intends to return all excess free cash flow, net of strategic uses, to shareholders through the end of fiscal year 2028.

## INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

- **Vera Rubin**: A high-performance computing platform including GPUs and custom CPUs designed for large-scale AI workloads.
- **Grace Blackwell**: NVIDIA's predecessor AI architecture optimized for large language model training and inference.
- **ACI&E**: A revenue subsegment representing AI Clouds, Industrial & Enterprise customers.
- **Sovereign AI**: Infrastructure and artificial intelligence capabilities developed by nations to maintain data sovereignty and national security.
- **Neocloud**: Cloud service providers focused exclusively on specialized AI-accelerated computing infrastructure.
- **Agentic AI**: Artificial intelligence systems capable of autonomous reasoning and executing multi-step workflows.
- **MLPerf**: An industry benchmark suite used to evaluate the training and inference performance of AI hardware and software.
- **DSX POD**: A modular reference architecture for data centers that integrates compute, networking, and cooling into ready-to-deploy containers.
- **BlueField-4**: A data processing unit designed to handle networking, security, and storage tasks to offload the main CPU.
- **NVLink Fusion**: A high-speed interconnect technology used to combine different processors into a single, unified computing unit.
- **Token**: The fundamental unit of data, such as a word or character, that an AI model processes for training or inference.
