## Summary

Alphabet's second quarter 2026 revenue grew 24% year-over-year, driven by AI investments in Search, YouTube, and Cloud, with Cloud revenue up 82%.

The company announced new AI models, Gemini 3.6 Flash and 3.5 Flashlight, and reported high demand for AI infrastructure, with a Cloud backlog of $514 billion.

Google Services revenue increased 15% to $95 billion, with strong growth in Search and YouTube ads, while Cloud operating income more than tripled year-over-year.

Alphabet is updating its CapEx guidance to $195-$205 billion for 2026, reflecting increased capacity investments to meet AI demand.

Management emphasized strong AI-driven momentum across Search and YouTube, with plans to expand AI capabilities and accelerate model releases, including the ambitious Gemini 4.
